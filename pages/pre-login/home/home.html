SuperProfile

SuperLeads

Manage enquiries and grow admissions

  • Bring parent enquiries from connected sources into one place.
  • Assign owners, schedule visits and track admission stages.
  • Follow up with messages sent through SuperConnect.

SuperHR

Hire staff. Manage attendance and payroll.

  • Publish school jobs and track applicants through hiring.
  • Manage staff records, attendance and leave.
  • Review salary calculations, deductions and payslips.

SuperSocial

Create, schedule and manage social media

  • Draft school posts and captions with AI assistance.
  • Review, schedule and publish to connected social accounts.
  • Promote admissions, showcase activities and track engagement.

SuperConnect

WhatsApp, email, SMS and calls. Together.

  • Manage conversations across connected channels.
  • Send announcements, reminders and personalised messages.
  • Keep message history beside enquiries and staff records.

SuperSite

Build your school website with AI

  • Generate a website draft from your school profile.
  • Customise pages, programmes and photos without coding.
  • Publish a mobile-friendly site with forms connected to SuperLeads.

SuperAI

AI tools for teaching and school operations

  • Choose tools for lesson plans, worksheets and school content.
  • Add your requirements to generate a relevant first draft.
  • Review, personalise and check the output before using it.
