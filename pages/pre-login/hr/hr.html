How SuperHR helps your school hire

SuperHR keeps job posts, applicants, interviews and follow-ups together, so hiring takes less of your day.

  • Post teacher jobs

    Post openings for PRT, TGT, PGT and leadership roles and keep every job on one Jobs page.

  • Track every applicant

    Move each applicant from applied to shortlisted, interviewed and hired.

  • Interviews and follow-ups

    Schedule interviews, set follow-up reminders and message candidates on WhatsApp, SMS or email.

  • AI match score

    Every applicant gets a match score for the job, so you see the best-fit teachers first.
