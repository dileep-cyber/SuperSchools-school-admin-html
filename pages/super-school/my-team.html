    Guideline

    1. Only Excel files (.xls or .xlsx) are supported. Upload one file at a time.
    2. Use the sample template below and keep the column headers and order unchanged.
    3. Name, Mobile Number, Role, and School Type are mandatory.
    4. School Type must be Day school, Preschool or Boarding. Separate more than one with a comma.
    5. Mobile numbers must be exactly 10 digits and contain only numbers.
    6. Role must match an available system role. You can’t add staff at the same level as, or higher than, your own role — for example, a School Administrator can’t add a School Owner.
    7. Department managers can only add roles allowed for their department and access level.
    8. Records with a matching name, mobile number, and email are flagged as duplicates.
    9. If any errors or duplicates are found, you can review or download the details before importing.

    Duplicates Detected

    Our system scans uploaded staff records to detect duplicates based on the following key identifiers:

    • Name: ensures every staff member is added only once.
    • Mobile Number: used for login access, so it must be unique across all staff.
    • Email: helps detect the same person added again under slightly different details.

    If matches are found across these fields, the records are grouped under "Duplicates Detected" so you can review and resolve them before importing.

    Click below to view all duplicate entries

    Unique Data

    The Unique Staff section lists every record that passed the duplicate check successfully. These staff are clean, accurate, and ready to be added to your staff list.

    What makes a record unique?

    • The name, mobile number, and email are all distinct.
    • No conflict exists with previously added staff records.

    By validating these key fields, the system ensures every staff member is distinct and avoids duplicate accounts.
